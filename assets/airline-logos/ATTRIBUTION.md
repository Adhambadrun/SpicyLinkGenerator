# Airline artwork

Brand marks are displayed solely to identify the selected airline; Spicy Link Generator
is not affiliated with or endorsed by these airlines. Trademarks remain with their owners.

- Most SVGs are from [Soaring Symbols](https://github.com/soaring-symbols/soaring-symbols),
  MIT-licensed code/data (see `SOARING-LICENSE.txt`); the project notes that trademark
  and original logo rights remain with the airlines. Assets were copied on 2026-10-12.
- `AA.svg` and `U2.svg` come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons)
  (CC0 asset compilation; airline trademarks are not licensed by CC0).
- If a carrier has no curated artwork, the UI shows its IATA code rather than silently
  borrowing a different airline's logo. These assets are not booking-link evidence.
